Security

How we protect your family's data, and how to tell us about a problem.

How your data is protected

  • Encryption: all traffic uses HTTPS/TLS, and data is encrypted at rest by Google Firebase.
  • Family isolation: server-side security rules let only members of your family read your family's data, with automated tests run before every release.
  • Server-calculated points: points are awarded by our servers, not the phone, so a kid can't approve their own chores or invent points.
  • Least data: no advertising ID, no location, no contacts; analytics never include names or task titles.
  • Verified accounts: new email sign-ups confirm their email before they can create or join a family.
  • This website: no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers, with a strict Content Security Policy.

Report a vulnerability

Email sameerrajwade@gmail.com with the details and steps to reproduce. Please give us reasonable time to fix it before disclosing it publicly, and don't access or change other families' data while testing. Our security.txt has the same details.
