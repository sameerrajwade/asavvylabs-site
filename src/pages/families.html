Families & safety

Built for kids, controlled by parents

Chore League is a family app first. Here is exactly how we keep it safe.

Parents are in charge

An adult creates the family. Younger kids are profiles the parent manages, with no login, email or phone needed.

No ads, ever

No ad networks and no advertising ID. Analytics never include names, task titles or reward titles.

No strangers

There is no chat and no public profile. Family leagues show only each family's league name and score.

Privacy-safe share cards

Share cards never show your family name. Standings show the top 3 by first name only.

No loot boxes

Bonus chests can't be bought, and show their point range up front. Nothing in the app costs money.

Kind by default

Missed-task penalties are off unless a parent turns them on. "Someone took the lead" alerts never go to kids under 10.

Chore League is designed to meet the Google Play Families policy and the US COPPA rule. See our privacy policy for the details, or delete your account and data any time.
