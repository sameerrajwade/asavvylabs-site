Support

How can we help?

Email us at {{config.supportEmail}}. A real person reads every message, usually within two working days.

Send feedback

In the app: Settings → Support → Send feedback. Ideas and bug reports are always welcome.

Lost access?

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen. Joined with Google? Sign in with the same Google account.

Not getting reminders?

Check Settings → Notifications, and allow Chore League to ignore battery optimisation in your phone's settings.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your Chore League account at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Chore League and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

By email

No longer have the app? Email {{config.supportEmail}} from the address you signed up with, and we'll delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, profile and photo.
  • If you're the family's last account holder: the whole family, including every profile, task, point, reward, sticker and photo.
  • Backups held by our infrastructure provider are purged on its routine cycle.
