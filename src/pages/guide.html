How Chore League works

A quick guide for parents, from setup to the first Sunday crowning.

{{screen:setup|Setup: chores suggested from your kids' ages}}

1. Set up your family

  1. Create your family and add your kids (name and age; no email or phone needed).
  2. Pick from the suggested chores and starter rewards, or add your own.
  3. Share your family code with the other grown-up so they can approve chores too.

Everything else, like family goals, perks and pocket money, can wait. Chore League brings each one back as a gentle card in week two.

{{screen:kidmode|Kid Mode: big buttons, only their own chores}}

2. Play the week

  • Kids tick off chores on their own phone or in Kid Mode on yours.
  • You get one notification and approve everything from the Approvals inbox.
  • Finishing all of today's daily chores opens the bonus chest.
  • Each week brings a new twist, announced the morning your week starts.
{{screen:winner|Sunday: the champion and this week's crowns}}

3. Crown a winner

When the week ends, Chore League ranks everyone, hands out the three crowns and starts a fresh week. Kids spend their points on rewards, or save up for a bigger one.

Want more? Start a family league and challenge friends over a 4-week season.

Tips from our test families

  • Keep it to 3–4 dailies per kid. Finishing everything should feel possible.
  • Approve once a day. An evening "approve all" keeps the game moving.
  • Let parents compete. Kids love beating a grown-up.
  • Use stickers. Praise for effort matters as much as points.
