Chore League ("we", "us") is a family chore game made by {{config.company}}. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. Contact: {{config.supportEmail}}.

Who uses Chore League

An adult account holder (a parent or guardian) sets up the family. Children usually take part as parent-managed profiles without their own login. An older child may join on their own phone with a family code a parent shares, with a limited child role.

Information we collect

  • Account: email address for account holders (email and password, or Google Sign-In).
  • Profiles: display name, optional age and optional photo for each family member, entered by the account holder.
  • Gameplay: tasks, completions, points, rewards, redemptions, stickers and settings.
  • Notifications: a push token and your device's time zone (not precise location), if you allow notifications.
  • Usage analytics: screen views and feature events with numbers only, never names or task or reward titles. We do not collect the advertising ID.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, browsing history or financial information. We do not use advertising SDKs and we never sell or share your data for advertising.

How we use information

Only to run the app: sign you in, sync your family's game, send the notifications you allow, and keep each family's data private to that family.

Storage and protection

Data is stored with Google Firebase, encrypted in transit and at rest. Server-side security rules let only members of your family read your family's data, and points are calculated on our servers. Google acts as our infrastructure provider (processor).

Children's privacy

Information about a child is provided and controlled by the parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13, show them ads, or profile them. A parent can view, edit or delete a child's profile at any time.

Your choices

  • Edit: manage profiles in the app (Family and Settings).
  • Notifications: switch each type off in Settings → Notifications.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete my account, or see Delete your account.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account exists and delete it when you delete your account.

Changes

We'll update the date above and tell you in the app about material changes.
