Features

Everything in Chore League

A real game for the whole family, with the parent tools that keep it fair and quick.

The game

Weekly winner

Points reset every week on the day you choose. Sunday night ranks the family and celebrates the champion.

Three crowns

Top scorer, most improved and goal crusher, so a younger kid can win a crown too.

Weekly twist

A new twist every week: double points for chores, early-bird bonus, team week (kids vs grown-ups) and more.

Daily bonus chest

Finish every daily task and a bonus chest opens with extra points. A perfect week opens a bigger one.

Streaks with a shield

Daily streaks pay milestone bonuses, and a streak shield saves one missed day.

Comeback boost

Whoever finished last week gets a small boost on every task, so nobody gives up.

Levels and perks

Levels climb with every point earned. Attach real-world perks you choose, like a later bedtime on Fridays.

Bounties

Post a one-off job like "wash the car, +100". First to finish wins it.

Family leagues

Challenge up to five other families over a 4-week season, scored fairly by average points per player.

For parents

One approvals inbox

Task claims and reward requests together. Approve all in one tap, or send one back with a quick note.

Trust levels

Let small tasks approve themselves, and ask for a photo only where it matters.

Starter setup by age

Add your kids' ages and get age-right chores and rewards. Change anything; add your own.

Optional pocket money

Show points as cash and approve payouts you make yourself. The app never holds money.

Weekly parent report

Who did what, approval times, and whether the game is still working for your family.

Game rules your way

Week start day, who competes, missed-task penalties (off by default), vacation pause.

For kids

Kid Mode

Hand over your phone: kids see only their own chores, tick them off and spend their points. A grown-up gate keeps settings safe.

Stickers

Parents send stickers for great effort. They never cost or earn points; they're just praise.

Teen style

A calmer look for ages 12 and up: no confetti bursts, plain wording.

Chore League

Do chores. Win the week. A free family chore game. No ads.
